Who we are

All types of care,
every step we share.

Srinika Medisurge is a Lucknow-based doorstep healthcare service founded by Dr. Piyush Saxena. We bring doctors, certified nurses, physiotherapists and hospital-grade equipment to your home — so elderly, bedridden and post-surgery patients don't have to face long hospital queues for essential care.

From a single injection or catheter change to round-the-clock nursing and ICU-level respiratory support, every visit follows sterile hospital protocols and is supervised by our medical team.
