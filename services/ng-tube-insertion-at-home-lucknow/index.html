Who Needs This Service in Lucknow?

Visiting a hospital OPD or clinic in Lucknow for non-emergency medical procedures causes immense physical strain for frail patients and their family members. This doorstep service is ideal for:

  • Stroke or neurological patients with dysphagia (inability to swallow) at high risk of choking or pulmonary aspiration.
  • Advanced dementia, Alzheimer's, or Parkinson's patients who cannot consume adequate nutrition orally.
  • Comatose or semi-conscious bedridden patients requiring enteral liquid feeding and crushed medication delivery.
  • Patients whose existing Ryle's tube has become dislodged, pulled out accidentally, or blocked by thick feeding formula.
  • Patients recovering from head and neck surgeries requiring temporary enteral feeding support.

What's Included in Every Visit

We follow strict hospital clinical hygiene protocols. Every doorstep visit comes completely equipped with:

  • Medical-grade silicone or PVC Ryle's nasogastric feeding tube with radio-opaque line (sizes 12Fr to 18Fr).
  • Sterile lubricating water-soluble jelly / local anesthetic spray for comfortable, trauma-free nasal passage.
  • Gold-standard placement confirmation: Whoosh test auscultation via stethoscope, gastric juice aspiration pH check.
  • Medical hypoallergenic tape securement on nose bridge to prevent accidental displacement.
  • Thorough demonstration to caregivers: How to check position before feeds, formula consistency, flushing with warm water.
  • Guidance on preventing tube blockage, oral hygiene care, and head elevation (30-45 degrees) during feeding.

Frequently Asked Questions (Ryle's Tube / NG Tube Insertion)

Our certified nurses follow strict dual verification: (1) Air insufflation auscultation ('whoosh test') using a stethoscope over the epigastrium, and (2) gastric aspirate verification. Feeding is only started after 100% clinical confirmation.
Do not attempt to reinsert it yourself, as blind reinsertion by untrained persons can cause tracheal trauma. Call Srinika Medisurge immediately at +91 96530 01359 for rapid reinsertion at home. If the patient develops coughing, breathing distress, choking, or severe pain, call 108 or proceed to the nearest emergency room immediately.
Standard PVC tubes should be replaced every 10 to 14 days. High-grade silicone feeding tubes can remain safely for 3 to 4 weeks with proper daily flushing.
Always flush the tube with 20-30 ml of warm drinking water before and after every feed and after giving crushed medicines. Our visiting nurse will demonstrate the exact flushing technique.
