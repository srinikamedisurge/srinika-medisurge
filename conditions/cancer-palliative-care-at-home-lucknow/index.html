Cancer Patient & Palliative Care at Home in Lucknow

A patient with advanced illness is usually most comfortable at home, among family, but home care becomes heavy when injections, dressings, tubes and round-the-clock help are needed. Srinika Medisurge supports families in Lucknow with nurses and attendants who carry out the care the treating oncologist and doctor have advised, gently and on time.

What Care Does This Patient Usually Need?

  • Injections and IV fluids as prescribed by the treating doctor
  • Dressing of wounds, ports or pressure sores in sterile conditions
  • Feeding tube and urinary catheter care when needed
  • Help with hygiene, turning, feeding and comfort through the day and night
  • Oxygen support at home when breathing is difficult
  • A doctor to call for questions between hospital visits

How Home Care Works

1

Follow the treating team

We work from the oncologist's and hospital's instructions and never change cancer treatment. Please share the latest reports and prescription.

2

Plan comfort care

Nursing visits and attendant shifts are set up around the patient's needs, with timing for injections, dressings and rest.

3

Care for the family too

Attendants take on the heavy daily tasks so family members can rest and spend time with the patient.

4

Know when to go to hospital

If symptoms change, the nurse informs the doctor and family quickly so the patient reaches the treating hospital in time.
