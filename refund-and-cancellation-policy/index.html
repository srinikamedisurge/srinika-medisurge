Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Srinika Medisurge

At Srinika Medisurge, we strive to deliver compassionate, prompt, and reliable medical care. We understand that medical schedules and patient conditions can change rapidly. Our refund and cancellation policy is outlined below.

1. Online Doctor Video Consultation (₹500 Fee)

  • Cancellation by Patient: If you cancel your online consultation at least 30 minutes before the scheduled time slot, a 100% full refund will be processed to your original payment method.
  • Doctor Unavailability: If our doctor is unable to connect due to an unforeseen emergency, you may choose to reschedule or receive an immediate 100% refund.
  • Post-Consultation: Once an online consultation has taken place and a digital prescription has been issued, the consultation fee is non-refundable.

2. Doorstep Medical & Nursing Visits

  • Cancellation Prior to Dispatch: If a scheduled home visit is cancelled before our medical staff departs the dispatch hub, no cancellation fee applies.
  • Cancellation After Staff Arrival: If staff arrives at your residence and the procedure is declined or cancelled, a nominal conveyance assessment fee (₹200) applies to cover travel expenses.
  • Completed Clinical Procedures: Fees for completed clinical procedures and opened single-use sterile consumable packs are non-refundable.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds via Razorpay are typically credited to the patient's original bank account or card within 3 to 5 business days, in accordance with standard Indian banking settlement cycles.

4. Assistance & Queries

For refund queries, please call our 24/7 helpline directly at +91 96530 01359 or Dr. Piyush Saxena at +91 89319 85484 or send a message on WhatsApp at +91 96530 01359.
